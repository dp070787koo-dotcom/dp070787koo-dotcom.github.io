Terms of use

Effective date: 14 October 2026.

Personal use

autposting-backup is an owner-operated personal encrypted backup workflow using restic, rclone and Google Drive. It is not offered as a commercial service and does not provide registration, subscriptions or storage to third parties. This website only describes the workflow.

Owner responsibilities

The owner authorizes access to their own Google account, uses only data they are entitled to back up, safeguards credentials, encryption passwords and recovery material, monitors storage quota and backup failures, and verifies restoration independently. The existing Desktop OAuth client and drive.file scope must be preserved during recovery to access the same application-created files.

Availability and recovery

Storage availability, API limits and account access depend on Google and the owner’s systems. Successful upload is not a guarantee of successful restoration. Recovery requires the correct restic password, repository and intact data. This informational website does not guarantee uptime, preservation or recovery, and does not execute backups or accept credentials.

Permissions and costs

Authorization and consent are performed by the owner directly with Google. Access may be revoked at any time. These pages introduce no fee or paid plan. The workflow uses the owner’s existing Drive quota and does not automatically purchase storage, enable billing or increase OAuth permissions.

Stopping use and contact

The owner can stop use, revoke OAuth access and remove backups as described in the privacy policy. For questions, use the owner’s support email on the application’s Google OAuth consent screen. Google, restic and rclone remain subject to their respective applicable terms and licenses.
